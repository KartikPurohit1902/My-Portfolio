About Dholera SIR

India's Most Ambitious Smart City is Being Built Right Now

Dholera Special Investment Region

Dholera SIR is India's first and largest planned Greenfield Smart City, spanning 422 sq km in Gujarat. Backed by the Government of India under DMIC, it is the centrepiece of India's industrial future.

  • 422 sq km master planned city — larger than Mumbai's island city
  • DICDL SPV: 50% Govt of India + 50% Govt of Gujarat ownership
  • Platinum rated industrial smart city designation
  • 6 town planning zones covering all development types
  • India's first semiconductor fab city hub

Strategic Location — Gateway to India

Located 100 km south of Ahmedabad on the Gulf of Khambhat, at the intersection of the DMIC corridor, Western Freight Corridor, and major Gujarat ports.

  • 100 km from Ahmedabad — 45 min via new expressway
  • 150 km from Vadodara (Baroda)
  • Near Pipavav, Dahej & Kandla ports for export
  • Planned metro rail connecting Ahmedabad–Dholera

From Ancient Port to Future City

  • 2009 — Gujarat SIR Act passed, Dholera declared SIR
  • 2012 — Master Plan (2010–2040) sanctioned by GIDB
  • 2016 — DICDL SPV formed by Govt of India & Gujarat
  • 2020 — ABCD building completed, city's administrative HQ
  • 2025 — Expressway 98% complete; Airport Phase 1 done
  • 2026 — Tata Semiconductor SEZ officially notified April 9

Legal & Title Clarity — Zero Risk

  • NA (Non-Agricultural) converted — legally buildable
  • NOC obtained from all relevant govt authorities
  • Title clear with no encumbrances or disputes
  • Unit plan passed by DICDL/GIDB
  • Registered sale deed with Sub-Registrar's office
  • RERA compliant projects where applicable

Everything you need to know before investing in Dholera SIR

What is Dholera Smart City and why is it unique?
+

Dholera SIR is India's first and largest planned Greenfield Smart City — 422 sq km in Gujarat, backed 100% by the Governments of India and Gujarat through the DICDL SPV. It sits on the DMIC corridor and is designed for 2 million residents, 800,000 jobs, and global manufacturing leadership. No other Indian city has the combination of government backing, DMIC connectivity, international airport, semiconductor hub, solar power, and greenfield master planning all in one place.

Is my investment legally safe? What documents do I get?
+

Absolutely. Every plot sold by DholeraInvestX is: (1) NA (Non-Agricultural) converted — legally buildable, (2) NOC cleared from all government authorities, (3) Title clear with verified ownership chain, (4) Unit plan passed by DICDL/GIDB, and (5) Registered sale deed executed with Sub-Registrar's office. This gives you completely documented, legally binding ownership that holds up in any court.

What is the minimum investment amount?
+

You can start investing from as little as ₹5–8 Lakhs for a 100 sq yd residential plot in TP-1/TP-3 zones. Industrial plots in TP-2/TP-4 start from ₹15–20 Lakhs for 200 sq yd. EMI/payment plans are available on select projects. Contact our team for the latest pricing and available inventory.

Can NRIs invest in Dholera plots?
+

Yes! NRIs can invest in Dholera plots under FEMA. Payment is made through NRE/NRO accounts. We have a dedicated NRI desk with experience handling investors from UAE, USA, UK, Canada, Singapore, and Australia. The registered deed process is handled with Power of Attorney if you cannot travel to India. We offer virtual site tours specifically for NRI investors.

What are the realistic return expectations for Dholera?
+

Historical data shows 4X return from 2019–2024 (₹2,000 → ₹8,000 per sq yd). Looking forward: Airport full operations, Expressway completion, Semiconductor plant construction (2026–2030), and Metro rail are all pending catalysts. Each catalyst has historically driven 30–60% price appreciation in comparable cities. We always recommend consulting a financial advisor and investing only discretionary capital.

The visit fee is 100% adjusted in your plot booking amount. Free site visits can be arranged from Ahmedabad if travelling independently — contact us to schedule.
