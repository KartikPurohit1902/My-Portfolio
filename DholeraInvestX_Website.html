THE PROJECT

What is Dholera SIR?

India's most ambitious urban development project — built from scratch

India's First Platinum-Rated Smart City

Dholera Special Investment Region (SIR) is India's first and largest planned Greenfield Smart City, located in Gujarat — approximately 100 km southwest of Ahmedabad on the Gulf of Khambhat.

Spanning 920 sq. km — twice the size of Mumbai — Dholera is being developed from scratch with world-class infrastructure, smart technology, renewable energy, and plug-and-play industrial facilities.

  • ✓ 920 sq km — 2x the size of Mumbai
  • ✓ 22 villages in Dholera Taluka, Ahmedabad District
  • ✓ Part of Delhi-Mumbai Industrial Corridor (DMIC)
  • ✓ Development Plan horizon: 2010–2040
  • ✓ Target: 2 million population, 8 lakh+ jobs

100% Government Backed — Zero Risk

Dholera is developed by DICDL — a Special Purpose Vehicle jointly formed by the Government of India (49% via NICDC Trust) and Government of Gujarat (51% via DSIRDA) on January 28, 2016.

  • ✓ Approved under Gujarat SIR Act 2009
  • ✓ DSIRDA — Regional Development Authority formed
  • ✓ All 6 TP Schemes sanctioned by GIDB
  • ✓ Environmental clearance from MOEF
  • ✓ Gujarat Budget 2026: ₹610 Cr allocated
  • ✓ Part of PM Gati Shakti National Master Plan

DMIC — India's $100 Billion Backbone

The Delhi-Mumbai Industrial Corridor is India's most ambitious infrastructure project. Dholera sits at the HEART of DMIC — connecting Delhi to Mumbai via industrial nodes.

  • ✓ $100 Billion+ DMIC project investment
  • ✓ Dholera is the largest node in the corridor
  • ✓ Japan-India joint infrastructure initiative
  • ✓ Multi-modal logistics connectivity
  • ✓ Road + Rail + Air + Port connectivity

Dholera: Ancient Port to Future City

Dholera has a rich history as an ancient port city on the Gulf of Khambhat. Now it transforms from a historic trading post to India's most futuristic smart city.

  • ✓ 2009: Gujarat SIR Act passed, Dholera declared
  • ✓ 2012: Master Plan (2010-2040) sanctioned by GIDB
  • ✓ 2016: DICDL SPV formed by Govt. of India & Gujarat
  • ✓ 2020: ABCD Building completed — city's HQ
  • ✓ 2025: Expressway 98% complete, Airport Phase 1 done
  • ✓ 2026: Tata Semiconductor SEZ officially notified
